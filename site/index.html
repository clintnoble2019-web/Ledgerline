Questions

Why is my number wrong in the first place?

Almost always missing data. When a tool cannot see where a coin came from, it assumes you paid nothing, and the whole sale price becomes gain. Move coins between your own wallets and it may record a sale on one side and a purchase on the other, which invents a disposal that never happened.

Does this replace Koinly?

No. You keep using whatever you already have. Ledgerline reads its export and points at the rows distorting the total, so you can fix them at the source.

Is my data safe?

The file is read by code running in your browser. It is not uploaded. Close the tab and nothing remains.

Is this tax advice?

No. Ledgerline is not a tax preparer and does not give tax advice. It reports inconsistencies in a file. What is reportable is your decision and your preparer's.

What if it finds nothing?

Then your export is clean and you owe us nothing. The free scan tells you either way.

Ledgerline flags rows in an export that inflate the gain column. A shared transaction hash is a split transfer. A wallet that goes negative after being positive is a missing inbound. A first sale with no history is an opening gap, not a diagnosis. Airdrops and rewards are ignored.

It does not recompute your return, it is not a tax preparer, and it is not tax advice.
